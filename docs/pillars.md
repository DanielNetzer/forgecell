# Pillars

1. **Compose** — Molecules from typed Atoms; Formulas define work.
2. **Execute** — Ticket → PROD is the ambition; current execution ends at a waiting human gate without shipping.
3. **Record** — Molecule ledger.
4. **Improve** — Ledger evidence → reasoned process change → human-approved Formula mutation → follow-up evaluation.
5. **Automate the automations** — Meta layer authors Formulas from Molecule evidence, including failures and limitations. Expected benefits must be tested.

The [learning contract](learning.md) explains the iterative research loop and current limits.
