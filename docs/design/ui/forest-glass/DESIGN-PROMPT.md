# Forgecell · Forest glass design prompt

Design one interactive local Lab screen for Forgecell. Use the supplied architecture-page reference only for material craft: dark atmospheric field, restrained glass frames, warm white cards, fine connectors, spacious type and role initials. Do not reuse Welltech branding, organization content, navy/lime palette, or its slide-based information flow.

## Brand and palette
- Forgecell four-dot diamond as an inline SVG; cream wordmark and mint mark.
- Field: forest-charcoal #101E1B, with deep forest #183C32 and blurred teal #246B60 blooms. Fixed background, subtle grain.
- Reading cards: warm cream #F8F6EF, forest ink #203C32, secondary #53665B.
- Text on glass: #F8F6EF; secondary #C2D1CA. Glass white 5.5%, border white 16%, blur22px and saturation140%, with an opaque fallback.
- Single functional accent: mint #A8E8CD. Reserve it for current selection, a human gate and its action. Human decision context is deep forest, not navy. Never rely on color alone.
- Instrument Sans 400–500 follows the existing Forgecell website; system monospace for identifiers and small labels. Keep labels readable and concise.

## Product and hierarchy
The whole screen is the Lab. No dashboard navigation, no chat, no team administration, no oversized page title.
A compact header identifies the local repository. A top shelf of cubes represents Molecules, each one ticket plus its ledger. Selecting a cube opens the run. The detail shows intake origin, exact Formula entry point, participating Atoms, their executor, context sources, behavior and evidence. A right pane shows the next action or Formula suggestion.
When no run is selected, show aggregate outcomes and measurement coverage. Do not invent cost values or claim success without evidence. Keep draft publication, merge and production verification separate.
Four locked primitives: Atom = workflow step; Molecule = ticket + ledger; Formula = durable YAML recipe; Lab = installed runtime. Learning changes the Formula process, not chat history or the codebase itself.

## Interaction
Clickable Molecules, native expandable Atom rows, YAML/ledger dialogs, contextual decisions and an overview state. Every interaction works by keyboard. Visible focus, predictable targets, native click semantics. Short state transitions only; reduced-motion and forced-colors fallbacks. No decorative perpetual animation. Use role initials only. Keep decoration outside reading areas.

## Prototype boundaries
Use explicitly labeled sample data. Decisions demonstrate feedback only: never execute, publish, activate a Formula or change a repository. Real approval binds to exact retained bytes and scope. No backend integration or live website deployment is implied by this study.

## Skill
Apply the tactile-ui and frontend-design skills. CSS glass is a visual approximation, not real optical refraction. Verify desktop, 400px, keyboard, overflow and readable planes; do not invent performance or usability evidence.

## Botanical Lab refinement
Reuse the marketing website's original pearlescent Blob sprite and botanical assets. Place the Blob studying a teal cube beside the Molecule shelf, with a subtle glass plinth. Keep ferns behind panel edges and outside all text. The shelf is a faint specimen grid: each selectable cube remains a Molecule, with its issue number and textual status below. Animate only Working specimens with a slow vertical float, small rotation and restrained inner glow. Preserve still states for waiting, recorded and blocked work. Include Pause motion, honor reduced motion, and pause animation when the page is hidden or the shelf is offscreen. On phones, use a two-column specimen grid and a smaller resident in its own footer space. No new product concepts or live execution implied.

## Dense Molecule grid
Use a seven-row contribution-style grid of 196 clearly labeled sample Molecules. One cell is one run, not a day. Only working cells pulse; selection has an outline, keyboard arrows navigate and Enter selects. Selecting again clears to aggregate overview. Formula name/version, Atoms and inline ledger follow selection. Keep the large overview and pause buttons removed; retain a small Motion checkbox. On mobile the grid scrolls within its own region, never the page.
